# 3. Visual System (1) — Mock MCQ

1. What is the approximate wavelength range visible to humans?
   a. 20–20,000 nm  
   b. 400–780 nm  
   c. 2–5 nm  
   d. 780–1,500 nm

2. Which relationship is correct?
   a. Blue light has shorter wavelength and higher frequency than red light  
   b. Wavelength and frequency increase together  
   c. Colour is independent of wavelength  
   d. Red light has shorter wavelength and higher frequency than blue light

3. Which structure performs most of the eye's refraction?
   a. Cornea  
   b. Optic nerve  
   c. Lens  
   d. Retina

4. Accommodation primarily involves:
   a. Crossing axons at the optic chiasm  
   b. Changing lens curvature to focus different distances  
   c. Moving the retina toward the cornea  
   d. Regenerating rhodopsin

5. In myopia, light from a distant object is focused:
   a. Behind the retina  
   b. In front of the retina  
   c. At the blind spot  
   d. On the optic nerve

6. Which pair develops from neural tissue and is therefore part of the CNS?
   a. Iris and lens  
   b. Retina and optic nerve  
   c. Cornea and retina  
   d. Cornea and lens

7. Why is the vertebrate retina described as inverted?
   a. The left visual field projects to the left cortex  
   b. Rods are concentrated in the fovea  
   c. Photoreceptors face the cornea directly  
   d. Light crosses ganglion and interneuron layers before reaching photoreceptors

8. Which property best distinguishes rods from cones?
   a. Rods are concentrated in the fovea  
   b. Rods support high-acuity colour vision  
   c. Rods are less light-sensitive  
   d. Rods support scotopic vision

9. A participant detects a very dim peripheral light after 25 minutes in darkness. Which mechanism contributes most?
   a. Cone bleaching  
   b. Rhodopsin regeneration in rods  
   c. Lens accommodation  
   d. Increased corneal curvature

10. Which photoreceptor is absent from the fovea?
   a. M-cone  
   b. S-cone  
   c. Rod  
   d. L-cone

11. Which cells connect neighbouring photoreceptors and contribute to lateral inhibition?
   a. Optic-nerve fibres  
   b. Ciliary cells  
   c. Horizontal cells  
   d. Ganglion cells

12. Retinal ganglion-cell axons collectively form the:
   a. Optic cup  
   b. Cornea  
   c. Optic nerve  
   d. Lens

13. Which description best fits a midget retinal ganglion cell?
   a. Large receptive field, transient response, poor colour selectivity  
   b. Small receptive field, sustained response, high spatial resolution  
   c. Large receptive field, high motion sensitivity, no centre-surround organisation  
   d. Small receptive field, single-photon detection, scotopic output

14. Which description best fits a parasol retinal ganglion cell?
   a. Fast, transient response and relatively low spatial resolution  
   b. Fine-detail processing with a very small receptive field  
   c. Exclusive input from S-cones  
   d. Slow, sustained, colour-selective response

15. For an ON-centre ganglion cell, light restricted to the receptive-field centre causes:
   a. No response  
   b. Reversal of the optic nerve  
   c. Increased firing  
   d. Decreased firing

16. White light covering both centre and surround of an ideal centre-surround cell produces:
   a. A colour afterimage  
   b. Maximal excitation  
   c. Maximal inhibition  
   d. Little net response

17. Blocking the retinal ON pathway with APB would most directly impair detection of:
   a. Light increments  
   b. Dark decrements only in daylight  
   c. Red-green boundaries only  
   d. Eye movements

18. Which method would most directly measure the spike rate of a parasol cell while its centre and surround are stimulated separately?
   a. Single-cell recording  
   b. Structural MRI  
   c. Lesion interview  
   d. fMRI

19. The convergence of roughly 126 million photoreceptors onto about 1.5 million ganglion cells implies:
   a. No loss of spatial information anywhere  
   b. One-to-one rod output  
   c. Expansion without compression  
   d. Considerable retinal signal compression

20. Centre-surround organisation is especially useful for:
   a. Regenerating photopigment  
   b. Measuring lens curvature  
   c. Enhancing contrast and edges  
   d. Producing accommodation

## Answers

1. b  2. a  3. a  4. b  5. b  
6. b  7. d  8. d  9. b  10. c  
11. c  12. c  13. b  14. a  15. c  
16. d  17. a  18. a  19. d  20. c
