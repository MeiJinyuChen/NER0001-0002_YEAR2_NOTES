
1. Which pathway in the brain is specialised for object recognition?  
a. Frontal Stream  
b. Dorsal stream  
c. Temporal stream  
d. Ventral Stream  
Correct Answer: d

---

2. Damage to which of the following areas could cause object agnosia, without any other visual processing impairment?  
a. MT/V5  
b. LGN  
c. IT  
d. LGN  
Correct Answer: c

---

3. Which stage follows the ‘input image’ stage in Marr’s Computation Theory of Vision?  
a. Template Matching  
b. Edge image  
c. 2 ½-D Sketch  
d. 3D Model  
Correct Answer: b

---

4. According to Marr and Nishihara, what are the primitives of human vision?  
a. Generalised cones  
b. Flat surfaces  
c. Zero crossings  
d. Points of light, or ‘Pixels’  
Correct Answer: a

---

5. Which piece of experimental evidence suggests human perception is not viewpoint invariant?  
a. IT neurones have been shown to respond equally to original objects and their simplified versions  
b. Many objects can be recognised as models of combined ‘primitives’  
c. Removing contour vertices impairs recognition more than removing mid-segments  
d. Objects with orientations interpolated between trained views are recognised better than orientations along orthogonal orientations  
Correct Answer: d

---

a. Selfridge’s Pandemonium Model
