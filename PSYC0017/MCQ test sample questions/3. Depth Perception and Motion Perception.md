
1. Which proposed signal is included in Helmholtz’s theory of eye movement, but not Sherrington’s?  
a. Efference copy of the ‘eye movement’ signal  
b. The retinal motion signal  
c. The ‘eye movement’ signal to muscle  
d. The ‘eye movement’ signal from muscle  
Correct Answer: a

---

2. What feature of the human visual system allows depth perception?  
a. Light-inverting lenses  
b. Horizontally separated retinas  
c. Multivariate responses of photoreceptors  
d. Cells with double-opponency  
Correct Answer: b

---

3. Which of the following is not a pictorial cue of depth?  
a. Motion parallax  
b. Relative size  
c. Linear perspective  
d. Atmospheric perspective  
Correct Answer: a

4. In binocular vision, when an object is further away from the observer than the point being fixated, it is said to have  
a. crossed disparity  
b. non-corresponding disparity  
c. corresponding disparity  
d. uncrossed disparity  
Correct Answer: d
