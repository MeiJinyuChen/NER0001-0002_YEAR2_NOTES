
1. What is the biochemical effect of a photon of light hitting a photoreceptor?  
a. All-trans-retinal is converted to 11-cis-retinal which depolarises the cell  
b. 11-cis-retinal is converted to all-trans-retinal, which hyperpolarises the cell  
c. 11-cis-retinal is converted to all-trans-retinal, which depolarises the cell  
d. All-trans-retinal is converted to 11-cis-retinal which hyperpolarises the cell  
Correct Answer: b

---

2. Which brain area has been attributed to the processing of colour memory?  
a. Lateral Occipital Cortex (LOC)  
b. V2  
c. Primary Visual Cortex (V1)  
d. Lateral geniculate Nucleus (LGN)  
Correct Answer: a
