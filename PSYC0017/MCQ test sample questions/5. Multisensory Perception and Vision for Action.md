
1. In the Maximum likelihood Model, the fused estimate has:  
a. broader reliability than either individual cue  
b. higher reliability than either individual cue  
c. lower reliability than either individual cue  
d. narrower reliability than either individual cue  
Correct Answer: b

---

2. The double flash illusion has been used as evidence against which theory of sensory integration:  
a. Maximum likelihood model  
b. Modality appropriateness hypothesis  
c. Cue reliability model  
d. Visual dominance theory  
Correct Answer: d

---

3. Neurons in the subcortical superior colliculus have been shown to have a supper-additive response because:  
a. the response is different whether auditory or visual stimuli are presented  
b. the response to both cues is equal to the sum of the single cue responses  
c. the response to both cues exceeds the sum of the single cue responses  
d. the response is the same whether auditory or visual stimuli are presented  
Correct Answer: c

---

4. Synaesthesia is most common with:  
a. Uncommon audio-visual sequences  
b. Common alphanumeric sequences  
c. Uncommon alphanumeric sequences  
d. Common audio-visual sequences  
Correct Answer: b

---

5. Which of the following is used for an allocentric frame of reference?  
a. The floor  
b. The body

d. The head

6. The receptive field of a neuron is found in the area of the visual field to which the eye will saccade, where is this neuron?  
a. VIP  
b. AIP  
c. LIP  
d. MIP

7. Saccadic remapping:  
a. allows the saccade target to fall on the fovea after the eye movement  
b. is triggered by the feedback signal produced by the eye movement  
c. ensures a stable representation of the visual world  
d. occurs after each eye movement  
Correct Answer: c

8. The Ebbinghaus illusion is evidence for the dissociation between the dorsal and ventral streams because:  
a. The grasp aperture is affected by the illusion and visual perception is unaffected  
b. The grasp aperture is unaffected by the illusion and visual perception is affected  
c. The grasp aperture and visual perception are correct for both circle arrangements  
d. The grasp aperture and visual perception are incorrect for both circle arrangements
