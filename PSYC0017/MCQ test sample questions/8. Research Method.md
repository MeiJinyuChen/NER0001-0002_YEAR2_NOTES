
1. What changes are measured by functional magnetic resonance imaging?  
a. Gamma rays emitted by a positron-emitting radionuclide  
b. Action potentials fired by individual neurons  
c. Release of neurotransmitters at the synaptic junction  
d. Blood-oxygenated level dependent response  
Correct Answer: d

----

2. Which of the following pairs describes two types of thresholds used in psychophysics experiments? 
a. Absolute and detection  
b. Subjective and objective  
c. Detection and discrimination  
d. Difference and discrimination  
Correct Answer: c

-----

3. What are the main problems with the psychophysics Method of Limits?  
a. Errors of habituation and anticipation  
b. Errors of subjectivity and objectivity  
c. Errors of subjectivity and habituation  
d. Errors of anticipation and objectivity  
Correct Answer: a

---

4. You are testing someone on a psychophysical task, and you ask the question “Are these stimuli different?”. What are you measuring?  
a. A discrimination threshold using a forced choice procedure  
b. A detection threshold using a forced choice procedure  
c. A detection threshold using a yes/no procedure  
d. A discrimination threshold using a yes/no procedure  
Correct Answer: d
