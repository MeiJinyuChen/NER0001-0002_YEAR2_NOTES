## Molecular Mechanism of LTP and LTD

| ------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **High Ca²⁺:**<br>→ Kinases  <br>→ LTP            | **Low Ca²⁺:**<br>→ Phosphatases (PP1 = protein phosphatase 1 OR calcineurin)<br>→ LTD |


### LTP
#### Comparison between early and late LTP

|                     | Early LTP                       | Late LTP                                                    |
| ------------------- | ------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------- |
| Duration            | Minutes to hours                | Hours to years                                              |
| Translational State | Post-translational              |                                                             |
| Mechanism           | AMPAR insertion/phosphorylation | Gene transcription<br>Protein synthesis<br>Synaptic tagging |

#### Molecules involved in PSD modulation

| Type                                                         | Mechanism                                                      |
| ------------------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------- |
| PKA                                                          | AMPA receptor insertion                                        |
| Tyrosine kinase<br>(MAPK = mitogen-activated protein kinase) | Gene transcription<br>→ Structural plasticity                  |
| Tyrosine kinase (SRC)                                        | Modulates NMDA receptor function<br>→ Enhances Ca²⁺ signalling |
| PKC                                                          | AMPA receptor phosphorylation<br>→ ↑ Conductance               |
| PKMζ                                                         | Sustain long-term AMPAR presence                               |
