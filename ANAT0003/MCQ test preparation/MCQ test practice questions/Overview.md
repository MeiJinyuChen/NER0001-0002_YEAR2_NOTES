## Answers and revision points

| Question | Answer | Key point                                                                                                                                                                |
| -------- | ------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| 1        | c      | The brainstem is a morphological, not functional or embryological, grouping: medulla, pons and midbrain.                                                                 |
| 2        | b      | Corticospinal fibres cross at the pyramidal decussation, producing contralateral control.                                                                                |
| 3        | d      | Nuclei gracilis/cuneatus → internal arcuate fibres → decussation → medial lemniscus → thalamus.                                                                          |
| 4        | a      | In open medulla the central canal opens to the fourth ventricle; the inferior olivary nucleus provides major input to the cerebellum.                                    |
| 5        | b      | Pontine nuclei relay a copy of motor-cortical information through pontocerebellar fibres and the middle cerebellar peduncle to cerebellar cortex.                        |
| 6        | c      | Inferior colliculus: audition; superior colliculus: vision; both integrate other modalities.                                                                             |
| 7        | d      | Most cranial nerves arise from multiple brainstem nuclei; CN X supplies parasympathetic efferents to most viscera.                                                       |
| 8        | c      | Reticular formation comprises diffuse brainstem networks and broadly projecting neuromodulatory systems.                                                                 |
| 9        | d      | Major cerebellar cortical inputs are from pontine nuclei and inferior olive; deep nuclei are its main outputs.                                                           |
| 10       | b      | Hypothalamus surrounds the third ventricle and connects differently to posterior versus anterior pituitary.                                                              |
| 11       | a      | Suprachiasmatic nucleus: circadian rhythms.                                                                                                                              |
| 12       | b      | LGN relays vision, MGN audition, and VPL (ventroposterolateral nucleus) somatosensation.                                                                                 |
| 13       | a      | VL integrates cerebellar and basal-ganglia inputs for feedback to primary motor cortex.                                                                                  |
| 14       | c      | Caudate + putamen = striatum; with globus pallidus they are the cerebral nuclei.                                                                                         |
| 15       | a      | Dopamine from substantia nigra modulates the GO/NO-GO circuit; its loss shuts down GO.                                                                                   |
| 16       | b      | Primary motor cortex occupies precentral gyrus and is Brodmann area 4.                                                                                                   |
| 17       | d      | Layer IV receives thalamic input; layer V supplies extra-cortical output and includes motor-cortex Betz cells.                                                           |
| 18       | a      | Corpus callosum is the commissural white-matter connection between hemispheres.                                                                                          |
| 19       | a      | Corticospinal/pyramidal system controls skilled voluntary movement; corticobulbar is its cranial/facial-directed counterpart.                                            |
| 20       | c      | General sensation has three synapses; olfaction bypasses the thalamus and reaches olfactory cortex directly.                                                             |
| 21       | b      | Developmental sequence in the notes: ectoderm → neural plate → neural folds/groove → neural crest → neural tube.                                                         |
| 22       | a      | Prosencephalon forms telencephalon and diencephalon; mesencephalon remains midbrain; rhombencephalon forms metencephalon (pons/cerebellum) and myelencephalon (medulla). |
| 23       | b      | Rhombomeres are seven Hox-patterned segments of the embryonic hindbrain that help specify hindbrain and cranial-nerve tissues.                                           |
| 24       | a      | Otic placode is associated with CN VIII and gives rise to auditory/vestibular structures for hearing and balance.                                                        |
| 25       | b      | Geniculate, petrosal and nodose placodes are the epibranchial placodes associated with CN VII, IX and X sensory ganglia.                                                 |
| 26       | a      | The cerebral aqueduct connects third and fourth ventricles; substantia nigra and VTA are dopamine-producing regions; lateral lemniscus conveys vestibular input.         |
| 27       | c      | Superior peduncle: midbrain/mainly output; middle: pons/mainly input; inferior: medulla/spinal cord/mixed.                                                               |
| 28       | a      | Recognise the rostral medulla set: vestibular, vagal, hypoglossal, solitary and raphe structures.                                                                        |
| 29       | a      | The internal medullary lamina partitions each thalamus into anterior, medial and lateral parts.                                                                          |
| 30       | a      | The trigeminal sensory nucleus replaces the dorsal horn at caudal medullary level.                                                                                       |
