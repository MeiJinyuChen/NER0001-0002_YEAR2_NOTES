1. **b** — A sulcus is a cortical groove; a gyrus is the intervening fold.
2. **e** — The insula lies deep to the frontal, parietal and temporal opercula.
3. **d** — Association fibres remain within one hemisphere; commissural fibres cross hemispheres.
4. **e** — The SLF is a long frontal–parietal/occipital association system.
5. **a** — The inferior longitudinal fasciculus links occipital and temporal cortex for ventral-stream functions.
6. **a** — The uncinate joins orbitofrontal and anterior temporal regions.
7. **d** — The cingulum provides this limbic-cortical linkage.
8. **c** — The corpus callosum is the main neocortical commissure.
9. **d** — The slides note no direct commissural connection for primary visual cortex (also for the hand somatosensory representation).
10. **a** — Forceps major connects occipital poles; forceps minor connects frontal poles.
11. **b** — Temporal interhemispheric links are mainly through the anterior commissure.
12. **b** — The posterior commissure supports bilateral pupillary light reflex circuitry.
13. **c** — Corona radiata fibres converge into the internal capsule.
14. **b** — The genu/posterior limb contain key descending motor and ascending thalamocortical fibres.
15. **c** — The right hemisphere is relatively mute and agraphic after callosal disconnection.
16. **e** — Isocortex is the six-layered cortex, unlike three-layered allocortex.
17. **c** — Brodmann parcellation is cytoarchitectonic.
18. **e** — VENs are the specialised large neurons emphasised in ACC and fronto-insular cortex.

11. **e** — M1 damage particularly impairs fine individual movements.
12. **d** — Lateral premotor cortex uses sensory cues to select and organise motor routines.
13. **a** — SMA is medial BA6 and is central to internally initiated actions.
14. **a** — SMA lesions may yield alien limb, utilisation behaviours and reduced spontaneous action.
15. **e** — Mirror-neuron responses bridge performance and observation of matching actions.
16. **c** — These are core executive-control functions of DLPFC.
17. **d** — OFC integrates sensory and emotional information to guide value-based decisions.
18. **c** — ACC is linked to autonomic, pain, conflict and error functions.
19. **b** — Insular damage can cause pain asymbolia: recognition without normal adverse valence.
20. **d** — The lecture rejects a single-function ‘consciousness centre’ account of claustrum.
21. **b** — Wernicke aphasia is fluent but poorly comprehended and often meaningless.
22. **d** — Right TPJ plus ventral PFC mediates reorienting to salient unexpected events.
