## Answers and review points

1. **b** — Magendie: dorsal roots are sensory and ventral roots are motor.  
2. **b** — Golgi’s silver stain made individual neural cells visible; Cajal used it to support the neuron doctrine.  
3. **c** — Hydra: nerve net/no specific spatial organisation; *C. elegans*: 302 spatially organised neurons.  
4. **b** — Neural plate is specialised ectoderm; it folds inward and its edges fuse dorsally.  
5. **b** — Dorsal alar fate is sensory; ventral basal fate is motor. This is neural-tube fate before peripheral axons are present.  
6. **a** — Prosencephalon becomes telencephalon and diencephalon.  
7. **d** — Metencephalon gives rise to pons and cerebellum; myelencephalon gives medulla.  
8. **c** — Mesencephalon contains the cerebral aqueduct; telencephalon/diencephalon/rhombencephalon correspond to lateral/third/fourth ventricles respectively.  
9. **b** — In the flatmap, ventral tissue is on the central line and dorsal tissue at the side edges.  
10. **a** — Seven rhombomeres contribute to medulla, pons and cerebellum (including cranial nerves); placodes form cranial nerves and associated receptors.  
11. **a** — Later forebrain, especially cortical, expansion drives the characteristic shape and accompanies later sulcal development.  
12. **a** — Bending dissociates embryological and adult-posture axes.  
13. **b** — Rostro-caudal, dorso-ventral and medio-lateral are the preferred explanatory axes.  
14. **c** — Ventral horn is a key voluntary motor output, driven by descending fibres; dorsal horn receives somatic sensory input and projects rostrally.  
15. **b** — Local spinal circuits generate/control reflexes.  
16. **a** — CST: primary motor neocortex → ventral-horn neurons.  
17. **b** — DCML is the ascending dorsal sensory system; gracile is lower limb/lower thorax and cuneate begins at mid-thorax upward.  
18. **a** — The slide links regional grey matter to limb input/output and notes loss of remaining axons caudally.  
19. **b** — PNS is outside brain/spinal cord; a spinal nerve supplies a dermatome.  
20. **b** — ANS is involuntary visceral motor control; sympathetic and parasympathetic divisions have opposing effects and both use a two-neuron chain.
