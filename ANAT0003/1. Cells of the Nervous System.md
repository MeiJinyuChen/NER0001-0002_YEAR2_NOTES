
### Astrocytes
#### types
1. protoplasmic astrocyte (grey matter)
2. fibrous astrocyte (white matter)
3. Muller glia (retina)
4. Bergmann glia (cerebellum)
#### functions
1. Regulation of synaptic activity (buffer ions, clear glutamate via gtransporters)
2. Astrocyte-astrocyte communication
3. Blood Brain Barrier
4. Energetic support
5. Regulation of synaptogenesis
6. Interaction with microglia and oligodendrocytes

### Microglia
- ramified (surveillant stage) --microglial activation-- amoeboid (phagocytic stage)
- Derive from the yolk sack (not bone marrow). Microglial precursors migrate into developing CNS early in development.
#### functions
1. programmed cell death
2. phagocytosis
3. neuronal plasticity
4. synaptic pruning
#### result of dysfunction
1. inflammation
2. amyloid plaques
3. excessive synaptic pruning
