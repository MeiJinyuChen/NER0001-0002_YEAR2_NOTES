### Dura mater

- Tough, fibrous layer
- Bilayered: periosteal (lining skull) and meningeal (deeper)
- 2 layers normally fused but separate at points (forming meningeal dural folds)

<img src="assets/Pasted%20image%2020260222161343.png" width="475" alt="Pasted image 20260222161343">

#### Meningeal dural folds

Formed when periosteal and meningeal dura mater separate. Resulting in cranial cavaties.
- Falx cerebri
- Tentorium cerebelli

##### functions

1. Stabilise the brain during head mobvements
2. Drainage channels for venous blood

<img src="assets/3ae7a0a42cfae4cc8595ecb28d56d5b9.jpg" alt="3ae7a0a42cfae4cc8595ecb28d56d5b9">

### Arachnoid mater

- ''Spiderweb'' appearance attributed to trabeculae
- Upper part fused to dura mater
- Follows most major folds of brain tissue (e.g. gyri), but not sulci of cortex or cerebellum 
- Arachnoid granulations: Outpouchings of the arachnoid that push through the dura into dural venous sinuses. Drain CSF from brain and spinal cord back to the venous blood inside the skull.

<img src="assets/Pasted%20image%2020260222172711.png" width="550" alt="Pasted image 20260222172711">

#### functions

1. Cushioning layer around brain and spinal cord
2. Structual integrity and circulation of CSF

### Subarachnoid space

- Traversed by arachnoid trabeculae that connect arachnoid mater to pia mater
- Filled with CSF and blood vessels
- Surround brain and spinal cord completely
  Cranial nerves and spinal nerve roots pass through subarachnoid space as they exit the CNS

<img src="assets/Pasted%20image%2020260222173834.png" width="325" alt="Pasted image 20260222173834">

<img src="assets/Pasted%20image%2020260222174133.png" width="375" alt="Pasted image 20260222174133">

#### functions

1. Blood supply to neural tissue
2. Shock absorption

### Pia matter

- Very thin and fine membrane
- Tightly bound to surface of brain tissue  
- Follows brain surface closely (sulci, gyri, folia)

#### functions

1. Protect brain surface
2. Contact neural tissue
3. Support microvascular supply

## Ventricular system

<img src="assets/Pasted%20image%2020260228145644.png" width="350" alt="Pasted image 20260228145644">

<img src="assets/Pasted%20image%2020260228150317.png" width="349" alt="Pasted image 20260228150317">

<img src="assets/Pasted%20image%2020260228152625.png" alt="Pasted image 20260228152625">

- Connected system of CSF-filled cavities.
- Derived from the the lumen of the neural tube.
- Cerebral aqueduct (midbrain) connects 3rd and 4th ventricles.
- Central canal: Cntinuation of the ventricular systen into the spinal cord.

### Ventricles: brain landmarks

<img src="assets/Pasted%20image%2020260228153522.png" width="400" alt="Pasted image 20260228153522">

- Lateral ventricle: Horns associated with cerebral lobes
- Third ventricle:
  1. On midline between left and right thalamus and hypothalamus 
  2. Hypothalamic nuclei and circumventricular organs signalling to and from CSF
- Fourth ventricle: In hindbrain dorsal to pons, ventral to cerebellum

### Choroid plexus

A network of capillaries and specialized epithelial cells that produce and secrete most of the CSF in the CNS. Located within the ventricles of the brain.

#### functions:

1. CSF production: By filtering blood plasma and generating an osmotic gradient that draws water into ventricles 
2. Homeostasis: Helps maintain the chemical environment of the CNS 
3. Barrier role: Forms part of the blood-CSF barrier, regulating what enters CSF from blood (not BBB)

### Cerebral spinal fluid

<img src="assets/83a6a218fb0b27d913e27b3dd522e563.jpg" width="525" alt="83a6a218fb0b27d913e27b3dd522e563">

#### properties:

- ~ 150 mL
- Production rate: ~ 0.3 - 0.4 mL/min = ~ 500 - 600 mL/day

#### functions:

1. Buoyancy: reduces effective weight of brain 
2. Cushioning 
3. Chemical stability: maintains ionic environment optimal for synaptic transmission 
4. Waste removal

## Neurovascular unit

### Composition:

neurons, astrocytes, endothelial cells, pericytes, smooth muscle, microglia 

### Function: 

- regulates cerebral blood flow to match neuronal metabolic demand

## Blood brain barrier

<img src="assets/Pasted%20image%2020260302225920.png" alt="Pasted image 20260302225920">

### Composition: 

astrocytes, pericytes,  basement membrane (structural extracellular matrix proteins), endothelial cells with tight junctions

### Function: 

- stabilises ionic and chemical environment 
- limits toxins and pathogens 
- separates central from peripheral immune signals
