| Structure                          | Main Function                                                                                                                                                                             | Primary Afferent Connection (Input)                                                                        | Primary Efferent Connection (Output)                                                                              |
| ---------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Olfactory Nerve (CN I)**         | <font color="#245bdb">Sensory        </font><br>Olfaction (smell)                                                                                                                         | Olfactory receptor neurons in olfactory epithelium                                                         | Olfactory bulb → olfactory tract → primary olfactory cortex                                                       |
| **Optic Nerve (CN II)**            | <font color="#245bdb">Sensory</font><br>Vision                                                                                                                                            | Retinal ganglion cells                                                                                     | Optic chiasm → optic tract → thalamus (LGN) → visual cortex                                                       |
| **Optic Chiasm**                   | Partial decussation of visual information                                                                                                                                                 | Nasal retinal fibres from both eyes                                                                        | Optic tracts carrying contralateral visual field information                                                      |
| **Oculomotor Nerve (CN III)**      | <font color="#245bdb">Motor (parasympathetic)</font><br>Eye movements (except for lateral / downwards movement); parasympathetic control of pupil (constriction) and lens (accommodation) | Oculomotor nucleus and Edinger–Westphal nucleus (midbrain)                                                 | Extraocular muscles (SR, IR, MR, IO); levator palpebrae; ciliary ganglion → sphincter pupillae and ciliary muscle |
| **Trigeminal Nerve (CN V)**        | <font color="#245bdb">Mixed (sensory, motor)</font><br>Facial sensation; mastication                                                                                                      | Face, oral cavity, teeth, meninges → trigeminal sensory nuclei (mesencephalic, pontine, spinal trigeminal) | Trigeminal motor nucleus + Supratrigeminal nucleus → muscles of mastication                                       |
| **Abducens Nerve (CN VI)**         | <font color="#245bdb">Motor</font><br>Lateral eye movement; gaze stabilisation                                                                                                            | Abducens nucleus (caudal pons)                                                                             | Lateral rectus muscle                                                                                             |
| **Facial Nerve (CN VII)**          | <font color="#245bdb">Mixed (sensory, motor, parasympathetic)</font><br>Facial expression; taste (anterior 2/3 tongue); lacrimation; salivation                                           | Taste fibres → nucleus solitarius; facial sensory input; facial motor nucleus receives cortical input      | Muscles of facial expression; lacrimal, submandibular and sublingual glands                                       |
| **Glossopharyngeal Nerve (CN IX)** | <font color="#245bdb">Mixed (sensory, motor, parasympathetic)</font><br>Taste (posterior 1/3 tongue); salivation; swallowing; visceral sensation (chemoreception, baroreception)          | Taste and visceral afferents → nucleus solitarius                                                          | Stylopharyngeus muscle; parotid gland; pharyngeal structures                                                      |
| **Vagus Nerve (CN X)**             | <font color="#245bdb">Mixed (sensory, motor, parasympathetic)</font><br>Parasympathetic control of thoracic and abdominal viscera; swallowing; visceral sensation; voice                  | Visceral sensory and cardiovascular information → nucleus solitarius                                       | Dorsal motor nucleus of vagus → thoracic and abdominal viscera                                                    |
| **Hypoglossal Nerve (CN XII)**     | <font color="#245bdb">Motor</font><br>Tongue movement                                                                                                                                     | Hypoglossal nucleus (medulla)                                                                              | Intrinsic and extrinsic tongue muscles                                                                            |

Cranial parasympathetic fibres:
- Emerge from nuclei in the brainstem
- Travel in 4 cranial nerves (3,7,9,10):
  Oculomotor (III)
  Facial (VII)
  Glossopharyngeal (IX)
  Vagus (X)
- Reach ganglia located in organs of the head, thorax and abdomen
