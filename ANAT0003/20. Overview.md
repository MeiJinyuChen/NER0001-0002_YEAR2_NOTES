### Rhombomeres

- Segmented subdivisions of the embryonic hindbrain
- Divide rhombencephalon into 7 rhombomeres (r1–r7)
- Visible as bulges
- Each has unique transcription factor patterns (especially Hox genes)
  → Determine fate of tissues in pons, cerebrllum and medulla (inc. cranial nerves)

### Placodes

- Localised ectodermal bulges outside of neural tube
- Give rise to cranial nerves and associated sensory receptors

| Placode                                                                      | Cranial Nerve / Structure Associated | Major Adult Derivatives                                                  | Primary Function                                  |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------- |

| **Trigeminal placode**                                                       | CN V (Trigeminal)                    | Trigeminal sensory ganglia (ophthalmic and maxillomandibular components) | Somatosensation from face                         |
| **Geniculate placode**                                                       | CN VII (Facial)                      | Geniculate ganglion                                                      | Taste (anterior 2/3 tongue), facial sensory input |

# Gross Anatomy of Brainstem

## Midbrain

### Superior Colliculus

- Two DA-producing areas (Substantia nigra + Ventral tegmental area)
- Crus cerebri
- Red nucleus
- Edinger-Westphal nucleus
- Oculomotor nucleus
- Leminiscus (spinal + medial + trigeminal)

### Inferior Colliculus

- Superior cerebellar peduncle
- Cerebral aqueduct:
  Linking 3rd and 4th ventricle
- Periaqueductal grey
- Leteral lemniscus:
  Convey input from vestibular nuclei

## Pons

- 4th ventricle
- Middle cerebellar peduncle
- Pontine nuclei:
  Major input to cerebellum (relay infor from motor cortex to cerebellum through pontocerebellar fibres)
- Pontocerebellar fibres:
  Connects pontine nuclei to middle cerebellar peduncle

| Peduncle                         | Connects Cerebellum To | Main Function            |
| -------------------------------- | ---------------------- | ------------------------ |
| **Superior cerebellar peduncle** | Midbrain               | Mainly output (efferent) |
| **Middle cerebellar peduncle**   | Pons                   | Mainly input (afferent)  |
| **Inferior cerebellar peduncle** | Medulla/spinal cord    | Mixed input/output       |

## Medulla

### Rostral Medulla

- Dorsal surface of the 4th ventricle
- Structures related to cerebellum function:
	- Inferior olivary nucleus (major output to cerebellum)
	- Inferior cerebellar peduncle (major brainstem-cerebellum fibre tracts)
- Vestibular nucleus
- Dorsal motor nucleus of vagus
- Hypooglassal nucleus
- Nucleus solitarius, Solitary tract
- Nucleus raphe magnus

### Mid Medulla

- Internal arculate fibres →(decussate)→ Medial lemniscus
  (derived from nuclei cuneatus and gracilis)

### Caudal Medulla

- Decussation of pyramids
- Nucleus cuneatus, Nucleus gracilis
- Dorsal horn is replaced by trigeminal sensory nucleus

| Peduncle     | Main connection                 | Main direction             |
| ------------ | ------------------------------- | -------------------------- |
| **Superior** | Midbrain                        | mainly **efferent**        |
| **Middle**   | Pons                            | mainly **afferent**        |
| **Inferior** | Medulla / spinal-related inputs | **mixed**, mainly afferent |
