
## Terminology
- **Isochizomers**: Resctriction enzymes that originate from different species that have the same recognition site and generate identical ends.
- **Neoschizomers**: Restriction enzymes that have the same recognition site but generate different ends.
- **Isocaudomers**: Restriction enzymes that generate identical ends but have different recognition sites.

Restriction enzymes are 5 times more efficient in linear lambda DNA than in circular plasmid DNA because of supercoiling. Circular DNA is cupercoiled, so it is more difficult for restrition enzymes to recognise and act on recognition sites.

## Methylation
![[685eb9054e56324d0b946d6f15f3dbc8.jpg]]
- Methylation prevents the locating and acting of REase, thus prevents the bacterial DNA from being cut by the REase defence mechanism. 
  If the methylation sequence and recognition sequence of a dam/dcm sensitive enzyme coincide cutting at all site is prevented, if they overlap cutting is only prevented at some sites.

## Star activity
Cutting at sites resembling the recognition sequence (e.g.GAATTC => AATTC) 
**Factors** 
- Increased concentration of glycerol→Best ≤ 5% some enzymes (EcoRI, BamHI ≤2%) 
  REase are often stored in buffers containing high concentration (usually 50%) of glycerol to prevent ice formation and stabilized at -20 °C
- pH too low/high (different buffer sets) 
- Ionic strength too low/high (different buffer sets, salts not washed off  clearly in purification step) 
- Divalent cofactor other than Mg<sup>2+</sup> (Mn<sup>2+</sup>, Cu<sup>2+</sup>, Co<sup>2+</sup>, Zn<sup>2+</sup>) 
- Organic solvents (Ethanol, DMSO, Dimethylformamide, volume excluder (e. g. ethylene glycol) 
  To precipitate and wash very small amount of unwanted DNA/RNA. Influence the stringency of REase if not washed away / dried properly.
- Prolonged reaction time 
- Too much REase

## Calculation
### How often does a REase cut (in bp)
e.g. BbeI
![[capture_20251229165901043.bmp|175]]
The length of the recognition site for BbeI is 6 bp. There is a 1000,000-bp DNA of random sequence.
- Probability a given 6-bp sequence matches the recognition site = (1/4)<sup>6</sup> = 1/4096
- Expected average distance between cut sites = 4096 bp
- Expected number of cut sites at DNA = 1000,000/4096 = 244
### How many PstI is required to cut 1 ug of pUC19
![[capture_20251230024200050.bmp|300]]
- One unit is the amount of restriction enzyme necessary to completely digest 1µg of λ DNA, in 1 hr, in a 50µl reaction volume and at a specified temperature.
![[capture_20251230031625340.bmp]]
- Amount of REase required depends on the molecular concentration of substrate and the number of cutting sites.
- Given that the quality of DNA are the same but the length of DNA are different, the molecular concentration of pUC19 relative to λ = 1 / (length pUC19 / length λ) = length λ / length pUC19
- A unit of PstI required to cut pUC19 = 1u / μg * relative ratio of molecular concentration * relative ratio of cutting sites = 1u / μg * (length λ / length pUC19) * (sites in pUC19 / sites in λ)
