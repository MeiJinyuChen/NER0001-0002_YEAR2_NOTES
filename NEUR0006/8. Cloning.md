
## Gibson assembly
Cloning (joining multiple overlapping DNA fragments into a single DNA) in a single isothermal reaction without restriction enzymes, regardless of fragment length or end compatibility.
![[a9824a37cce6f13a2e70215b700dfbb8.jpg]]
### Process
~50°C in a single tube
DNA fragments used are designed so that their ends have homologous sequences (typically 15-40 base pairs)
1. Cutting
   Cut the DNA with T5 exonuclease, forming complementary 3' single stranded overhangs (T5 exonuclease is unstable at 50°C and exposes 3' end of DNA after some time)
2. Annealing complementary termini
3. Gap filling
   The gaps are filled by Phusion High Fridelity DNA polymerase
4. Nick-sealing
   The DNA backbone is sealed by Taq DNA ligase
### Enzymes
1. T5 exonuclease
2. Phusion High Fridelity DNA polymerase
3. Taq DNA ligase
