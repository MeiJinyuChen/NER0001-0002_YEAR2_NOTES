## Paokaryotes

### Start site

#### Shine-Dalgarno sequence

=ribosome binding site (RBS)
Certain part of consensus sequence that is complementary to and binds a part of the ribosomal small subunit DNA. Join ribosome to the correct frame to read the DNA. 
Recognises the 3' end of the ribosome.
Usually GGAGG

#### Consensus sequence

5'-UAA**GGAGG**U-3'

AUG - anticodon of fMet - tRNA<sub>f</sub><sup>Met</sup>

## Eukaryotes

### Kozak sequence

helps the ribosome recognize the correct start codon after it has already bound to the mRNA. Not in the same sense as the Shine-Dalgarno sequence.
5'-**A**CC<u>AUG</u>**G**-3'
The start codon is embedded inside.
