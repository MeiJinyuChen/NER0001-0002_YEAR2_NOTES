## Nutrient stress/starvation pathway

Involved mechanism: S6K, 4E-BP1, ULK complex, TFEB.

<img src="assets/capture_20251209153931648.bmp" alt="capture_20251209153931648">

### Protein synthesis

1. When amino acids are plentiful: mTORC1 is activated at the surface of lysosomes.

<img src="assets/capture_20251209143942755.bmp" alt="capture_20251209143942755">

2. Active mTORC1 stimulates protein translation via at least 2 mechanisms involving phosphorylation.
   -  Active mTORC1 phosphorylates <span style="background:#fff88f">S6 kinase</span>, increasing its activity, which activate <span style="background:#fff88f">protein synthesis</span>.
   - Avtive mTORC1 phosphorylates <span style="background:#fff88f">4E-BP1</span>, which stops binding to <span style="background:#fff88f">eIF4E</span>. It increases<span style="background:#fff88f"> protein synthesis</span>.

<img src="assets/capture_20251209150652645.bmp" alt="capture_20251209150652645">

3. Amino acid starvation reduces mTORC1 activity, leading to reduced protein synthesis.

### Autophogy regulation

#### ULK complex

1. Active mTORC1 inhibits <span style="background:#fff88f">autophagy</span> initiation by phosphorylating <span style="background:#fff88f">ULK complex</span>

<img src="assets/capture_20251209151229728.bmp" alt="capture_20251209151229728">

2. ULK1 is dephosphorylated and activated under amino acid starvation. It rapidly stimulates autophogy initiation.

<img src="assets/capture_20251209152000439.bmp" alt="capture_20251209152000439">

#### TFEB (transcription factor EB)

1. Active mTORC1 also phosphorylates and inhibits transcription factor EB (<span style="background:#fff88f">TFEB</span>). TFEB is involved in <span style="background:#fff88f">lysosome biogenesis and autophagy</span>.

<img src="assets/capture_20251209152331625.bmp" alt="capture_20251209152331625">

2. TFEB is dephosphorylated and activated under amino acid starvation. It moves to the nucleus, <font color="#c00000">increasing the expression of lysosome & autophogy gene</font>. The degradative capability of cell is greatly boosted during starvation.

<img src="assets/capture_20251209152352742.bmp" alt="capture_20251209152352742">

## Heat stress pathway

1. <span style="background:#fff88f">HSF1</span> normally binds to chaperones.

<img src="assets/capture_20251209154424467.bmp" width="450" alt="capture_20251209154424467">

2. Under heat stress, misfolded proteins build up. Chaperones go to bind the misfolded proteins. HSF1 is released from the bound chaperones.
   HSF1 is trimerised and activated (in some of the cases, HSF1 does not require trimmerisation to be activated), and then move to the nucleus.

<img src="assets/capture_20251209154449166.bmp" alt="capture_20251209154449166">

3. Active HSF1 induces expression of more chaperones including Heat Shock Proteins (HSPs) e.g. Hsp70.

<img src="assets/capture_20251209154724741.bmp" alt="capture_20251209154724741">
