## Different protein-encoding strategies for prokaryotes and eukaryotes

**Prokaryotes: Polycistronic transcription**
- Many related genes are transcribed together from one promoter, forming one mRNA.
- This cluster of genes + single promoter is called an operon.
  Operon → one promoter → multiple genes → one mRNA → multiple proteins.

**Eukaryotes: Monocistronic transcription**
- Each protein-coding gene is controlled by its own promoter, producing one mRNA per gene.
- Eukaryotes do not use operons (with extremely rare exceptions).
Monocistronic gene organization → many promoters → each gene transcribed independently → one protein per mRNA.

### Mechanism

#### No lactose:

- LacI binds operator
- RNA polymerase blocked
- Operon OFF

#### Lactose present:

- Allolactose binds LacI
- LacI dissociates
- Operon ON at low level

#### Low glucose:

- cAMP rises
- CAP binds promoter (CAP site)
- Strong transcription activation
